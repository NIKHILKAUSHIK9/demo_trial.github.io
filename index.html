Gaia DR3 Pipeline

Photometric & Kinematic Study of Young Open Star Clusters

This application provides a comprehensive pipeline for analyzing young open star clusters using Gaia DR3 data. It covers membership probability determination via Gaussian Mixture Models, isochrone fitting with extinction correction, stellar evolutionary stage labeling, kinematic analysis in Galactocentric/Heliocentric coordinates, and Initial Mass Function derivation.

Data filtration parameters include parallax bounds ($\sigma_\pi/\pi$), proper motion selections ($\mu_{\alpha*}, \mu_\delta$), G-band photometric errors, and Renormalized Unit Weight Error (RUWE) thresholds to ensure high-quality astrometric solutions from the Gaia catalog.
